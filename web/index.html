What is inside

Everything a money month in India actually needs

Not a dashboard for a hypothetical user — the screens that came out of tracking real salary days, UPI splits and "bhai, kab lautayega?".

Transactions & accounts

Income, expense and transfer between cash, bank, UPI wallet and credit card. Categories, tags, merchant, notes and payment method. Soft delete with undo.

Budgets with your cycle

An overall budget plus per-category limits, on a monthly cycle that can start on the day you actually get paid.

Insights

Daily, weekly and monthly trends and a category breakdown — drawn as plain SVG from local SQL aggregates.

Recurring payments

Rent, subscriptions and credit-card bills with due and overdue tracking, month-end clamping and one-tap confirm.

Salary tracking

Expected day, one-tap "credited", plus partial, bonus and delayed states — with a history per month.

People & money

A personal lending ledger: who you lent to or borrowed from, partial repayments, due dates and follow-ups by call, SMS or WhatsApp.

Investments

Mutual funds, FDs, RDs, PPF, stocks, gold — contributions, manual valuation, gain or loss, allocation and maturity dates.

Savings goals

Targets with scheduled contributions, suggested amounts and progress that only moves when you actually save.

Smart reminders

Morning and evening nudges whose text is composed from your own data: due lendings, salary, SIPs, goals, today's spend.

App lock

A 4–6 digit PIN. On Android it lives in the Keystore and re-locks after 30 seconds in the background.

SMS auto-tracking Android

Bank and UPI alerts are parsed on the phone into a review inbox — approve to add, dismiss to ignore. OTPs and promos are skipped. On the web, paste the alert text instead.

AI summary, your key

Optional monthly narrative via OpenRouter with an API key you bring. Only aggregates leave the device — never notes or transaction lists — and the app never lets AI do the arithmetic.

Privacy, specifically

What leaves your device: nothing, unless you switch it on

Always local

  • Transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, people, salary, investments, goals
  • SMS text in the review inbox (Android) — parsed on the phone, never uploaded
  • Your PIN — in the Android Keystore; on the web, only a salted hash

Only if you opt in

  • AI summary — sends pre-computed totals and category shares to OpenRouter with your key. Never notes, never a transaction list.
  • Cloud backup (Android) — to a Supabase project you control.

Honest about the web

Browser storage is less protected than a phone's keystore: anyone with access to your browser profile can read it. The web app says so where it matters, and keeps API keys encrypted at rest. For the strongest protection, use the Android app.

Same code, two homes

Web vs Android

One React Native codebase. The web build swaps the native pieces for browser ones and is upfront about what it cannot do.
